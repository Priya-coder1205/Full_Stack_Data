About IMA

The Indian Military Academy (IMA) in Dehradun is a premier officer training institution for the Indian Army. Established in October 1932, it transforms young civilian and cadet recruits into professional military leaders dedicated to serving the nation. Located in the foothills of the Himalayas, the academy has trained thousands of officers who have proudly served India.

"The Safety, Honour and Welfare of your Country Come First, Always and Every Time."
